The Idea

A different register

Most magazines for men either shout or sneer. Sovrano does neither. It treats culture, style, history and dating with equal seriousness and equal pleasure — and assumes you are old enough to want both.

We assume the reader is curious. Everything else follows from that.

It is printed to be kept, not scrolled — an object that earns its place on a shelf and improves the room it sits in. Quarterly, on good paper, edited slowly and read at leisure.

The Four Pillars

Four subjects, taken seriously

  • I

    Culture

    Film, food, music and ideas — understood deeply rather than reported breathlessly.

  • II

    Style

    Style, not fashion. The principles behind the choices, useful for a decade.

  • III

    History

    Narrative non-fiction that reads like a thriller and teaches like a seminar.

  • IV

    Dating

    Honest, adult writing about desire and courtship — and taking women seriously as people.

The Muse

One woman, one story

Each issue is built around a single woman, cast as the lead of a cinematic story set in that issue's theme. The photography is provocative through narrative and suggestion — never nudity, and always with her agency at its centre.

It is the difference between a portrait and a poster: a woman who is the author of the scene, not its decoration.

First Issue

Issue 01 — Rome, 1962

Postwar Rome, mid-stride: the dolce vita at its glittering height, a city reinventing itself in good tailoring and better company. A story about appetite, ambition and the art of arriving — told the way it deserves to be.
